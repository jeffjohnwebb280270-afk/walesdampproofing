Three problems, one symptom

Damp is a diagnosis, not a product.

Gall ôl llanw ar deras yn y Rhondda fod yn bedwar peth gwahanol. Mae cwrs atal lleithder wedi'i chwistrellu yn trwsio un ohonynt yn union. Dyma'r hyn rydym yn ei wahanu wrth arolygu.

Groundwater rising by capillary action

Rising damp

Genuinely uncommon, and over-diagnosed for fifty years. It stops at a horizontal tide line, usually under a metre, and carries ground salts that bloom through the plaster. On solid stone walls the cause is more often a raised external ground level, a bridged course, or a blocked sub-floor vent than a failed one.

Water crossing the wall from outside

Lleithder treiddiol

The most common cause of a damp Welsh wall, and the one this country produces at scale. Cracked cement render on a rubble stone wall, failed pointing, a split downpipe, a slipped slate, a defective chimney flashing. It appears at the point of failure and worsens after rain rather than following the seasons.

The law changed on 1 April 2026

Social landlords in Wales are now on the clock.

The Welsh Housing Quality Standard was updated with effect from 1 April 2026 and now sets fixed timescales for hazards including damp and mould. It applies to councils and housing associations. Private landlords are not on a clock — but they are bound by a fitness duty that never switches off.

Read the duties in full

WHQS damp & mould timescales

  1. 24ORIAU

    Significant risk, harm likely imminent

    Investigate within 24 hours, and carry out the work needed to remedy it within a further 24 hours.

  2. 10DIWRNODAU GWAITH

    Significant risk, harm not imminent

    Ymchwilio o fewn 10 diwrnod gwaith, yna unioni o fewn 5 diwrnod gwaith arall.

  3. 5DIWRNODAU GWAITH

    If it cannot be finished in time

    Provide the contract-holder with a written summary plan within 5 working days setting out what will happen and when.

Social landlords must also publish their response times and report performance to the Welsh Government. General guidance only — see the landlords page.

Why we charge for the survey

Ymweliad gwerthu yw arolwg am ddim.

If the person diagnosing your wall is paid only when you buy the treatment, there is exactly one diagnosis they can afford to reach. That is how a great many Welsh terraces ended up with an injected damp-proof course they never needed, in walls where the real fault was cracked render or a broken downpipe.

We charge a survey fee and we tell you what is wrong, including when the answer is "nothing that needs us". You are free to take the report to any contractor you like. If you want us to carry out the works, the survey fee comes off the price.

Fees are quoted and confirmed in writing before we attend, along with your cancellation rights where the contract is agreed at your home.
