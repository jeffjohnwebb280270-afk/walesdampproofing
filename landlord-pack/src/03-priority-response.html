Every social landlord in Wales is working to the same clock, and there is a finite number of PCA-qualified damp and timber surveyors in the country. The organisations with capacity in the autumn will be the ones that arranged it in the spring.

What a standing arrangement gives you

  • A named response window. An agreed number of working days from instruction to attendance, so the WHQS investigation clock is a diary entry rather than a scramble.
  • A report format written to evidence the duty. Dated at every step — when you became aware, when we attended, what we found, what it needs — so the file itself shows you responded properly.
  • A plain-English written summary you can pass straight to the contract-holder, including where the five-working-day summary plan is required.
  • Agreed rates across your stock, so a survey does not need a quote each time.
  • One surveyor who knows your stock. The failure mode of a 1900 rubble terrace is not the failure mode of a 1960s cavity block, and knowing which you own shortens every visit after the first.

The condensation argument

"They're drying washing indoors" is the standard first response, and sometimes lifestyle genuinely is a factor. But condensation and mould are also caused by cold solid-wall surfaces, missing or broken extract ventilation, windows that will not open, poor insulation and heating that costs too much to run. Those sit on the landlord's side of the line, because they go to whether the dwelling is fit.

A survey that measures surface temperature and humidity settles that argument in writing — which is worth considerably more to a landlord than winning it verbally. For an agent it matters twice over: the compliance sits on the Rent Smart Wales licence, not just on the client relationship.

Point contract-holders at DampCheck Wales. It is free, we build and run it, and it helps them identify what they are looking at and report it properly — in writing and dated. An early, clear report is a good deal easier to scope than a phone call, and the date stamp protects both sides.
