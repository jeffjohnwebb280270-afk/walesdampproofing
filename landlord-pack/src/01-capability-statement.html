Why independence matters to you

If the person diagnosing a wall is paid only when the treatment is bought, there is exactly one diagnosis they can afford to reach. That is how a great many Welsh properties ended up with an injected damp-proof course they never needed, in walls where the real fault was cracked render or a broken downpipe.

You are free to take our report to any contractor you like. If you want us to carry out the works, the survey fee comes off the price.

What a survey includes

Internal moisture profile

Readings at skirting level, 300 mm and 1 m on every affected elevation, plotted so the pattern is visible rather than asserted. Surface readings are cross-checked with deep-wall probes, because a conductivity meter reads salt and foil as enthusiastically as it reads water.

Salt analysis

Nitrate and chloride testing on drilled samples. Ground salts confirm rising damp; their absence is frequently the single fact that saves several thousand pounds and a wall full of holes. It is the test most treatment quotes skip.

External defect inspection

Render condition and the cracks in it, ground levels against the wall, sub-floor ventilation, pointing, rainwater goods, chimney heads and flashings — and whether the wall is wearing a cement skin it was never designed to carry.

Air and surface conditions

Relative humidity, air temperature and surface temperature at the cold spots. On a solid wall with no cavity this is what separates condensation from moisture arriving through the fabric, and it is the measurement most free surveys skip entirely.

What you receive

  • A photographic report, room by room, with the diagnosis and the evidence behind it.
  • A schedule of works split into urgent, remedial and general maintenance — costed, so you can price it against anyone else's quote and hand the maintenance items to a joiner or roofer.
  • Where decay has reached joists, lintels or bearings, a plain statement that a structural engineer is required. We do not carry out structural design and we will not quote around it.
  • Timber work where it is warranted: masonry sterilisation, timber replacement and resin repair, and targeted insecticidal treatment only where an active infestation is confirmed. Historic infestation in dry timber needs no chemical treatment at all.
