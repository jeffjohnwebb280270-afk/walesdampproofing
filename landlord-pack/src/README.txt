WHAT IS IN HERE

  01  Capability statement
      Who we are, what a survey includes, what you get, fees and cover.

  02  WHQS damp & mould duties
      The 2026 timescales for social landlords, the continuous fitness duty
      for private landlords, and how Wales compares with England and
      Scotland. Written to be printed and pinned up.

  03  Priority response
      The standing arrangement — response window, agreed rates, and a
      report format built to evidence the WHQS duty.

  04  Damp & mould report form            PDF + Word
      For your front-line staff, at the point a case is first reported.
      The date on this form is the date the clock starts.

  05  Investigation record                PDF + Word
      One per case. Dated at every step, so the file itself shows the
      duty was met.

  06  Contract-holder summary letter      PDF + Word
      The written summary the WHQS requires within 5 working days where a
      case cannot be completed in time.

Documents 04 to 06 come both ways: use the Word version to add your own
logo and details and type into it, or print the PDF and fill it in by hand.
They are yours to adapt.


HOW THEY FIT TOGETHER

  A report comes in       ->  04  Report form           ->  your staff
  The clock starts        ->  05  Investigation record  ->  you and us
  The contract-holder
  is told in writing      ->  06  Summary letter        ->  you
